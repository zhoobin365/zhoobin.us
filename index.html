About

I am a Civil and Water Resources Engineer and Research Scientist specializing in the integration of satellite remote sensing, geotechnical analysis, and hydrologic modeling to study land subsidence, infrastructure–soil interaction, and hydrologic extremes. My research emphasizes InSAR-based deformation monitoring, scalable geospatial workflows, and the coupling of remote sensing observations with physics-based models.

Research Themes

InSAR & Land Subsidence

Urban subsidence monitoring, infrastructure aging, and deformation analysis using SAR time series.

Water Resources & Hydrology

Hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, flood forecasting, reservoir water balance, and cold-region river processes.

Geotechnical Systems

Subsurface investigations, tailings dam safety, slope stability, and integration of geotechnical data with remote sensing.

Geospatial Computing

Python-based automation, Google Earth Engine, cloud processing, and reproducible scientific workflows.

Selected Research Projects

Urban Land Subsidence Monitoring

Integrated InSAR time-series analysis with subsurface and infrastructure data to quantify deformation mechanisms in urban coastal environments.

River Ice & Cold-Region Hydraulics

Automated satellite-based monitoring of river ice dynamics combined with HEC-RAS simulations for flood risk assessment.

Tailings Dam Safety Assessment

Applied satellite deformation monitoring and water balance analysis to support geotechnical safety evaluation of mining tailings facilities.

Coupled Hydrologic–Hydraulic Modeling

Integrated HEC-HMS and 2D HEC-RAS modeling for watershed-scale flood forecasting and infrastructure impact analysis.
