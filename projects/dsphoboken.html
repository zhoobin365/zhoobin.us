InSAR-Based Urban Land Subsidence Monitoring

Study Area: Hoboken, New Jersey

Research Motivation

Urban coastal areas are vulnerable to land subsidence due to groundwater withdrawal, aging infrastructure, and subsurface conditions. This project integrates InSAR time-series analysis with geotechnical and hydrologic data to quantify deformation mechanisms in Urban coastal area of Hoboken, New Jersey..

Data & Methods

  • Sentinel-1 SAR (InSAR time series)
  • Geotechnical borehole and infrastructure data
  • Time-series deformation analysis
