InSAR-Based Monitoring for Tailings Dam Safety

Morenci, Arizona | Satellite Deformation Analysis & Risk Assessment

Project Overview

Satellite-based Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) was applied to detect and quantify millimeter-scale surface deformation at a tailings storage facility. The objective was to support early identification of instability mechanisms and provide long-term performance assessment using remote sensing techniques.

Engineering Context

Tailings storage facilities represent high-consequence infrastructure. Continuous monitoring is critical to detect progressive deformation, slope instability, and hydro-mechanical responses before failure conditions develop.

Data & Methods

  • Sentinel-1 SAR time-series processing (InSAR)
  • Millimeter-scale deformation velocity extraction
  • Integration with geotechnical borehole data
  • Piezometric head correlation analysis
  • Temporal trend evaluation for stability assessment

Key Outcomes

  • Identification of spatial deformation gradients across embankment zones
  • Detection of long-term settlement trends
  • Correlation between pore pressure variations and surface displacement
  • Demonstration of satellite monitoring as an early-warning support tool
