InSAR-Based Monitoring for Tailings Dam Safety

Study Area: Hoboken, New Jersey

Research Motivation

Satellite-based Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) was utilized to detect and quantify millimeter-scale surface deformation at tailings dam facilities, supporting early identification of instability mechanisms and long-term performance assessment.

Data & Methods

  • Sentinel-1 SAR (InSAR time series)
  • Geotechnical borehole and piezometric head
  • Time-series deformation analysis
